Tomato Time

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Tomato Time ("the App") is published on Google Play by TheStudio9. The app is developed under the Studio 9 brand. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" refer to TheStudio9.

Summary

Tomato Time does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. All focus statistics are stored locally on your device. No account is required.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Number of completed work sessions ("tomatoes")
  • Number of completed 2-hour cycles ("journeys")
  • Daily and weekly harvest statistics
  • Your sound on/off preference
  • In-progress timer state (so sessions continue after closing the app)

This data never leaves your device unless you back up your phone through your operating system's own backup service (e.g. Google Backup).

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No cloud sync or user accounts
  • No internet connection required for core functionality

Notifications

If you grant permission, the App schedules local notifications on your device to remind you when a work session or break ends. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are not sent to any server.

Permissions

  • Notifications — optional, to alert you at session and break transitions
  • Vibrate — optional, for notification vibration
  • Internet — used only by the app framework; the App does not send user data over the network

Children's Privacy

Tomato Time is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact TheStudio9 at: support@studio9.app
